# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in TerraQuorum a respectful, welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections when impact differs from intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Give actionable feedback in issues, reviews and discussions.
- Credit ideas, reports and contributions from others.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, repeated off-topic comments or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing secrets, private credentials or security vulnerabilities in public threads.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent bans from participating in the project.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers privately. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
